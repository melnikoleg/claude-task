## Assumptions
- Bus fare: 8 PLN per person per leg (round trip = 16 PLN/person), a standard regional single fare for a ~19 km PKS route in this area; no child discount applied (conservative - a discounted child fare with ID would lower this further). Exchange rate used: 1 EUR = 4.3625 PLN, mid-market rate dated 2026-09-21 (Wise).
- Site fee: 0 EUR, confirmed by multiple regional sources - the Dark-Sky Park is a free, always-open protected nature area; the only restriction is vehicle access (not relevant, party travels on foot/bus).
- Gear: only the headlamp from gear.md's Gaps to Fill table is costed; the binoculars and warm clothes are already owned (no cost); no telescope anywhere in the revised plan (mode is binoculars-only visual, confirmed in requirements.md, targets.md and gear.md), so no telescope line appears here.
- Food: no operator/vendor pricing exists for a self-prepared thermos and snack; the 1.50 EUR/person figure is a modest planning estimate for the much shorter ~4h13m-4h14m curfew-bounded session (one hot drink portion plus a snack per person), reduced from the prior ~3.70 EUR/person estimate that was sized for an ~11 h full-night session - roughly proportional to the ~2.5x shorter session length.
- Contingency: 10% of the 22.37 EUR subtotal (travel + site fees + gear + food), rounded up to 2.24 EUR.
- Total (24.61 EUR) is well under the 40 EUR limit, with 15.39 EUR headroom - no cuts are required.
