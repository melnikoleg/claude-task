# Target List - 2026-09-21-izera-visual

Site: Izera Dark-Sky Park (proxy coordinates Świeradów-Zdrój, lat 50.9092, lon
15.34309, elevation 465 m), UTC+1. Gear: 10x50 binoculars only - the telescope has
been dropped from requirements.md, so no eyepiece guidance or telescope-only targets
appear below.

Hard constraint from requirements.md: the session must start early and end by 22:30
(child curfew / last bus back to Szklarska Poręba), overriding darkest-window
optimization. Per night.md this leaves a pre-22:30 stretch of roughly 18:16-22:30
(4h14m) on 2026-11-07 (primary, ranked #1, 2.0% moon illumination) and 18:17-22:30
(4h13m) on 2026-11-06 (secondary, ranked #2, 5.7% moon illumination), both entirely
moon-free per night.md. All altitude figures below are from `object_visibility` for
both nights; times cited use the 11-07 track, with the 11-06 track confirmed within
one minute of the same altitudes throughout, so the plan holds on either night.

Two of the three requested targets - Jupiter and M42 (Orion Nebula) - do not clear the
20 degree altitude gate before the 22:30 curfew on either candidate night; both are
listed under Rejected Targets. M45 (Pleiades) does clear it and anchors the plan,
filled out with two further 10x50-friendly targets, Albireo and M31 (Andromeda
Galaxy), chosen because they are bright, high, and available in the same pre-22:30
window and because the sky is essentially moon-free all three nights, so faint
binocular deep-sky targets are not being displaced by a bright moon.

## Observing Order
Times below use the 11-07 track (primary night); the 11-06 track is within a minute
of the same altitudes throughout, so the same order and slot lengths apply if the
session falls on 11-06 instead.

1. **18:16-18:40 - Arrival and dark adaptation.** No target yet; 20-30 minutes for eyes
   to adapt before anything faint is attempted, as required.
2. **18:40-19:10 - Albireo.** Already past its 18:16 peak of 58.7 deg and sinking in the
   west-southwest (22.2 deg by 22:16); it is the one target that would be lost
   entirely if it were left for later, so it goes first while still high enough for a
   steady split.
3. **19:15-19:45 - Andromeda Galaxy (M31).** High (73-80 deg) and moon-free; placed
   after Albireo so eyes have had the full 20-30 minute adaptation window before
   attempting the faintest, averted-vision target of the night.
4. **19:50-20:20 - Double Cluster.** Climbing toward its own peak (64-72 deg in this
   slot, still rising to 83.6 deg later); bright enough for direct vision, a good
   "victory lap" after the harder averted-vision galaxy.
5. **20:30-22:30 - Pleiades (M45).** Still climbing (33-52 deg across this slot) and
   the target least sensitive to the exact curfew cutoff, so it fills the remainder of
   the session up to the hard 22:30 end time.

## Rejected Targets
- **Jupiter** - fails gate 5 (20 deg altitude for at least an hour inside the usable
  window). `object_visibility` shows Jupiter at -25 deg at the 18:16 dark-window start
  on 2026-11-07 (-25 deg on 2026-11-06), still below the horizon at 22:16 (-10.1 deg on
  11-07, -10.3 deg on 11-06), and not crossing 20 deg altitude until 01:16, over 2.5
  hours after the 22:30 curfew, on both nights. Jupiter's 2027 opposition is 11
  February 2027 (in-the-sky.org); as that date approaches Jupiter rises progressively
  earlier, so by January-February 2027 it will clear 20 deg well before any 22:30-style
  curfew. Not usable on any of the three candidate November 2026 nights.
- **M42 (Orion Nebula)** - fails gate 5. `object_visibility` shows M42 at 12.5 deg
  altitude at 22:16 and only crossing 20 deg at roughly 23:16-23:17 local on both
  2026-11-07 and 2026-11-06, 45-50 minutes after the 22:30 curfew. It would fit the
  curfew window once Orion rises earlier in the evening, roughly by December 2026 -
  January 2027.
