## Night Checklist

**Optics**
- 10x50 binoculars (owned) - primary instrument for all four targets (Albireo, M31, Double Cluster, Pleiades)
- Lens cloth for the binoculars - dew/condensation risk cannot be ruled out even though sky-forecast.md has no dew-point number yet (NOT-FORECASTABLE); cheap microfibre cloth, keep in a pocket, no purchase tracked here as most households already own one

**Power**
- Phone, fully charged before departure - required always
- Spare phone battery/power bank if available, carried in an inner pocket to stay warm - cold halves battery life; no confirmed temperature yet (sky-forecast.md NOT-FORECASTABLE), so this is precautionary rather than tied to a specific number

**Warmth**
- Warm clothes (owned) in full layers - confirm layer count once temperature numbers are available (see Owned Gear Check note)
- Hot drink in a thermos for the party, including the 9-year-old, to help sustain the ~4h13m-4h14m session ending right at the 22:30 curfew
- Extra blanket to sit/wrap in, since the session is short and back-to-back with no long idle stretch to warm up by moving around

**Safety**
- Red-light headlamp (gap above) - preserves night vision between target switches and lights the walking/bus route
- Fully charged phone (listed again here as the safety item: emergency contact and route-finding on the walk/bus leg)
- Confirm the return bus schedule or walking route back to Szklarska Poręba in advance - the session is timed to the 22:30 curfew and the last bus, not the darkest hour, so there is no slack for a late finish

**Comfort**
- Something to sit or kneel on (foam mat, folding stool, or a blanket from home) - short breaks between the four targets in the 4h14m window
- Snacks for the 9-year-old between target switches (Albireo -> M31 -> Double Cluster -> Pleiades), since the compressed timeline leaves little idle time - keep them ready rather than planned as a long game, unlike a longer session would allow
