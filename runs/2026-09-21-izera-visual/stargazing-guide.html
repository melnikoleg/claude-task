Targets

Observing order matches the timeline above, primary night 2026-11-07 (backup 2026-11-06 figures differ by at most 0.1° / 1 minute):

  1. Albireo - peak 58.7° at 18:16, sinking through the session; double star, warm golden-yellow primary next to a fainter steel-blue companion, split with a steady 10x50 hold.
  2. Andromeda Galaxy (M31) - peak 80.5° at 21:36; soft oval smudge with a brighter core, averted vision recommended.
  3. Double Cluster (NGC 869 / NGC 884) - peak 83.6° at 23:16 (still climbing through the session); two dense knots of pinpoint stars, direct vision, no averted vision needed.
  4. Pleiades (M45) - climbs from 15.1° (18:16) to 51.8° (22:16); tight knot of 6-7 bright blue-white stars with dozens of fainter companions filling the binocular field - best binocular target of the night.

Jupiter and Orion Nebula (M42) were requested but are not on this plan: both fail to clear 20° altitude before the 22:30 curfew on every candidate night (targets.md, Rejected Targets).
