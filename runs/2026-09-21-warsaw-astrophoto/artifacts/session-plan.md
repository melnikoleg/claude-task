# Stargazing Session Plan - 2026-09-21-warsaw-astrophoto

## Summary
Two adults drive from Warsaw to Bolimowski Landscape Park farmland edge (near Puszcza
Mariańska/Bolimów), an estimated 59 minutes one way, to photograph the Andromeda
Galaxy (M31) with a Canon R6, 24mm f/1.8 lens and tripod (no tracker), on the night of
**2026-10-16** (primary) or **2026-10-17** (backup). The Milky Way core, also
requested, is not observable from Warsaw's latitude in October - see Targets below -
so M31 is the sole imaging target. The plan is darkness- and moon-good on both
candidate nights, but the sky/weather verdict is **provisional, not a confirmed GO**:
Open-Meteo's forecast horizon does not yet reach these dates, so cloud cover must be
rechecked before departure (see Go/No-Go).

## Site
**Bolimowski Landscape Park farmland edge** (near Puszcza Mariańska/Bolimów).
- Coordinates: lat 51.97901, lon 20.35037, elevation 137 m
- Estimated drive: 59 minutes one way (53.1 km straight-line, road factor 1.3x at 70
  km/h), well inside the 90-minute limit
- Estimated Bortle ~4 (rural, ~53 km from Warsaw's centre, outside the city's light
  dome; not a measured SQM reading - treat as an estimate)
- Access: public roads run through the landscape park, open to traffic and parking at
  all hours; no entry fee, no gate. Landscape parks (unlike national parks) do not
  confine visitors to marked trails. Stay off marked hiking trails and out of the
  Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve within the park, where off-trail movement is
  restricted.
- Horizon: forest alternates with farmland and river-valley clearings; use an open
  agricultural field on the park's edge, not a forest clearing. The exact field was
  not scouted in person - arrive before dark to confirm a clear horizon and legal
  parking.
- Facilities: none on site; nearest fuel/toilets in Żyrardów or Skierniewice, a short
  drive away
- Backup site: Chrosna farmland, south of Celestynów (lat 52.0382, lon 21.45781), 41
  minutes one way, Bortle ~5, tree-screened from Chrosna's railway station lighting.
  Park on the road verge without blocking farm access.
- Risk noted by site-scout: October evenings can bring ground fog in the Rawka river
  valley near this site - check local fog risk on the night.

## Targets

### Andromeda Galaxy (M31)
- Magnitude 3.4; max altitude 79.4 deg at 23:34 local (2026-10-16 transit), 79.4 deg
  at 23:32 (2026-10-17 transit)
- Above 20 deg for 9.67 h on 2026-10-16 (essentially the whole dark window) and 10.0 h
  on 2026-10-17
- Exposure: 24mm f/1.8, R6 (5.98 micron pixel pitch), untracked - NPF rule gives 10 s
  max, ISO 3200, f/1.8. Shoot 80-120 subs (~15-20 min stacked integration) plus
  calibration frames
- At 24mm, M31 (roughly 3 x 1 deg) fills under 5% of the 74 x 53 deg frame - a small,
  soft, elongated grey smudge with a brighter core in a wide starfield next to
  Cassiopeia, not a close-up of the galaxy's disc or dust lanes

### Milky Way core - not a target this session
Requested by the observer, but geometrically unobservable from Warsaw's latitude (52
deg N) in October: `mcp__astro__object_visibility` for the galactic core returns a max
altitude of only 0.9 deg at 19:34 local on 2026-10-16 and 0.7 deg on 2026-10-17, with
0 hours above the 20-degree usability threshold on both nights. This is a seasonal
effect, not a site or weather problem - the core sets with the Sun in mid-autumn and
will not return to a useful evening altitude until spring. No site choice or clear sky
fixes this. The fainter northern Milky Way band (through Cassiopeia, Perseus, Cygnus)
is above the horizon and may be naked-eye visible at this dark site, but it is not a
tracked catalog object and is not planned as an imaging target.

## Gear Checklist
All gear owned; checklist-only mode (no purchases, 0 EUR gear spend).

**Optics**: Canon R6 + 24mm f/1.8 lens on the tripod, leveled at setup; lens hood if
owned; microfiber lens cloth (check for dew every 20-30 min - not a confirmed forecast
trigger, but standard October precaution); memory card(s) with room for 150-200+ RAW
frames plus a spare.

**Power**: fully charged battery, installed; keep it in an inner jacket pocket between
shooting blocks (cold roughly halves lithium-ion life per ~10 C drop); minimize
chimping/live-view to stretch the single battery across ~9.5 h (no spare battery
budgeted); phone charged for navigation, red-light backup and the night-before weather
recheck.

**Warmth**: layered clothing (base, insulating mid, windproof/waterproof outer), hat,
gloves (touchscreen-compatible), insulated boots; hot drink in a thermos for both
adults.

**Safety**: red-light headlamp; phone charged and carried, check signal on arrival;
basic first-aid if normally carried in the car.

**Comfort**: camp chair or folding stool; snacks for both adults across the ~9.5 h
session; compass/star app for orientation at setup if not using the phone.

No gaps: remote shutter release is not needed (10 s exposures fire via the R6's
built-in 2-second self-timer plus continuous drive) and a spare battery is handled by
the conservation workaround above, not a purchase.

**Rule**: if forecast cloud cover for the dark window exceeds 40% on the 24-hour
check for 2026-10-16, fall back to 2026-10-17 at the same site (or Chrosna, the
shorter-drive backup site). If both nights exceed 40% cloud, postpone the session -
do not drive out on a night the artifacts cannot confirm as clear. This plan is a
**provisional GO** pending that check, not a confirmed one.

## Safety
- **Cold**: October overnight sessions in open farmland run well past midnight; dress
  in layers, keep a hot drink, and watch each other for signs of cold exposure over
  the ~9.5 h session.
- **Drive home while tired**: departure is after an all-night session (~05:00-06:00
  arrival home); if either driver is too tired to drive safely, rest at the site or
  swap drivers before setting off.
- **Isolation**: the site has no facilities and no other people expected nearby;
  check phone signal on arrival, since it is the primary way to call for help or
  recheck weather.
- **Tell someone**: let a third party know the site, the night, and the expected
  return time before leaving home.
- **Wildlife/terrain**: farmland/forest-edge park at night - stay on public roads and
  out of the Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve; watch footing in the dark away from
  the vehicle.
