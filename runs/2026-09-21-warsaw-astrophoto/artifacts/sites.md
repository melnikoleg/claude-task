# Dark-Sky Sites - 2026-09-21-warsaw-astrophoto

No official Dark Sky Park lies within 90 minutes of Warsaw (Poland's three IDA-recognised
dark-sky parks - Izerski, Bieszczadzki, Bory Tucholskie - are all several hours away). Polish
amateur-astronomy forums agree that no site within roughly 30 km of the Warsaw city boundary is
truly dark; the three candidates below trade distance for sky quality within the 90-minute limit.
Sky-quality figures are not from a measured SQM reading at these exact points; they are estimated
from forum reports of local conditions plus general knowledge of the Warsaw light dome, and are
flagged as estimates throughout.

## Candidate Sites

### 1. Bolimowski Landscape Park farmland edge (near Puszcza Mariańska / Bolimów)

| Coordinates | lat 51.97901, lon 20.35037 |
| Elevation | 137 m |
| Bortle / sky quality | Estimated Bortle ~4 (rural, ~53 km from Warsaw's centre, well outside the city's light dome; no SQM source available for this exact point) |
| Straight-line distance | 53.1 km from start point |
| Estimated drive | 59 min (road factor 1.3x haversine at 70 km/h) |
| Horizon | Forest (~70% of the park) alternates with farmland and river-valley clearings (Rawka valley, glades such as Siwica); use one of the open agricultural fields on the park's edge rather than a forest clearing for a full horizon. Best-facing direction not independently confirmed - scout the specific field at dusk on arrival. |
| Access and legality | Public roads run through the landscape park and are open to traffic and parking at all hours under general Polish traffic law; landscape parks (unlike national parks) do not confine visitors to marked trails and have no documented dusk-to-dawn closure. Stay off marked hiking trails and out of the Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve within the park, where reserve rules restrict off-trail movement. No entry fee. |
| Facilities | None on site; nearest fuel/toilets in Żyrardów or Skierniewice, both a short drive away |

## Recommended Site

**Bolimowski Landscape Park farmland edge (Site 1)** is recommended: it is the darkest of the
three candidates by a clear margin on distance-from-Warsaw grounds, is still well inside the
90-minute limit at an estimated 59 minutes, and sits on public roads with no documented night
restriction. **Chrosna (Site 2)** is the runner-up - a shorter, 41-minute drive with a
reasonably dark, tree-screened field, useful as a fallback if the extra driving time to Site 1 is
undesirable or weather favours the closer site.

## Risks

- None of the Bortle/sky-quality figures above come from a measured SQM reading at the exact
  coordinates; they are estimates from forum reports and distance from Warsaw's light dome. Treat
  them as approximate.
- Site 1: the exact field to use was not scouted in person; arrive before dark to confirm a clear
  horizon and legal, unobstructed parking, and stay out of the Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve
  and off marked trails.
- Site 2: farmland is privately worked; park on the road verge, not across field access, and avoid
  the site during harvest/muddy conditions in case the verge is soft after rain.
- Site 3: the closest and easiest site but the brightest; useful only as a same-night backup if
  driving further is not possible, and its southern/south-western horizon (needed for the Milky
  Way) is the most light-affected of the three.
- October evenings in Poland can bring ground fog near the reservoir (Site 3) and in the Rawka
  river valley (Site 1); check local forecast fog risk on the night.
