Site

Bolimowski Landscape Park farmland edge (near Puszcza Mariańska/Bolimów).

  • Coordinates: lat 51.97901, lon 20.35037, elevation 137 m
  • Estimated drive: 59 minutes one way (53.1 km straight-line, road factor 1.3x at 70 km/h), well inside the 90-minute limit
  • Estimated Bortle ~4 (rural, ~53 km from Warsaw's centre, outside the city's light dome; not a measured SQM reading - treat as an estimate)
  • Access: public roads run through the landscape park, open to traffic and parking at all hours; no entry fee, no gate. Landscape parks (unlike national parks) do not confine visitors to marked trails. Stay off marked hiking trails and out of the Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve within the park, where off-trail movement is restricted.
  • Horizon: forest alternates with farmland and river-valley clearings; use an open agricultural field on the park's edge, not a forest clearing. The exact field was not scouted in person - arrive before dark to confirm a clear horizon and legal parking.
  • Facilities: none on site; nearest fuel/toilets in Żyrardów or Skierniewice, a short drive away
  • Backup site: Chrosna farmland, south of Celestynów (lat 52.0382, lon 21.45781), 41 minutes one way, Bortle ~5, tree-screened from Chrosna's railway station lighting. Park on the road verge without blocking farm access.
Risk noted by site-scout: October evenings can bring ground fog in the Rawka river valley near this site - check local fog risk on the night.

Targets

Andromeda Galaxy (M31)

  • Magnitude 3.4; max altitude 79.4 deg at 23:34 local (2026-10-16 transit), 79.4 deg at 23:32 (2026-10-17 transit)
  • Above 20 deg for 9.67 h on 2026-10-16 (essentially the whole dark window) and 10.0 h on 2026-10-17
  • Exposure: 24mm f/1.8, R6 (5.98 micron pixel pitch), untracked - NPF rule gives 10 s max, ISO 3200, f/1.8. Shoot 80-120 subs (~15-20 min stacked integration) plus calibration frames
  • At 24mm, M31 (roughly 3 x 1 deg) fills under 5% of the 74 x 53 deg frame - a small, soft, elongated grey smudge with a brighter core in a wide starfield next to Cassiopeia, not a close-up of the galaxy's disc or dust lanes

Milky Way core - not a target this session

Requested by the observer, but geometrically unobservable from Warsaw's latitude (52 deg N) in October: mcp__astro__object_visibility for the galactic core returns a max altitude of only 0.9 deg at 19:34 local on 2026-10-16 and 0.7 deg on 2026-10-17, with 0 hours above the 20-degree usability threshold on both nights. This is a seasonal effect, not a site or weather problem - the core sets with the Sun in mid-autumn and will not return to a useful evening altitude until spring. No site choice or clear sky fixes this. The fainter northern Milky Way band (through Cassiopeia, Perseus, Cygnus) is above the horizon and may be naked-eye visible at this dark site, but it is not a tracked catalog object and is not planned as an imaging target.

Safety

  • Cold: October overnight sessions in open farmland run well past midnight; dress in layers, keep a hot drink, and watch each other for signs of cold exposure over the ~9.5 h session.
  • Drive home while tired: departure is after an all-night session (~05:00-06:00 arrival home); if either driver is too tired to drive safely, rest at the site or swap drivers before setting off.
  • Isolation: the site has no facilities and no other people expected nearby; check phone signal on arrival, since it is the primary way to call for help or recheck weather.
  • Tell someone: let a third party know the site, the night, and the expected return time before leaving home.
  • Wildlife/terrain: farmland/forest-edge park at night - stay on public roads and out of the Puszcza Mariańska nature reserve; watch footing in the dark away from the vehicle.
