Site

Zootzen observation site, Sternenpark Westhavelland (Category 1 point), lat 52.786901, lon 12.609480, elevation 30 m. Straight-line distance 61.5 km from Berlin; estimated drive 69 minutes each way. Official, sign-posted, no gate, no fee, with an information kiosk and benches/table for public night use. The final ~300 m of the gravel access track is reserved for agricultural traffic — park where the public path narrows and walk in from there. No toilet, no lighting, no shelter beyond the car. Berlin's light dome sits low on the southeastern horizon; the clearest, darkest sky is north/northwest, away from it.

Targets

Ordered as in the timeline above.

  1. The Moon (naked eye, opportunistic) - thin crescent, 12 deg altitude, gone by ~20:01. Does not qualify as a built-around target (0 h above 20 deg both nights) but worth the first look.
  2. Andromeda Galaxy / M31 (binoculars) - max 78.6 deg at 19:21; elongated grey-white smudge with a brighter core; averted vision helps trace the halo.
  3. Perseus Double Cluster (binoculars) - max 85.5 deg at 21:01; two dense sparkling patches, easily resolved as two separate clusters in one binocular field.
  4. Pleiades / M45 (binoculars) - max 61.4 deg at 22:21; tight, sparkling blue-white cluster filling much of the field.
  5. Geminid meteor shower (naked eye, primary target) - radiant near Castor/Pollux in Gemini, high overhead by 02:00-03:00; effectively visible the whole dark window; expect real double-digit rates by late evening, climbing toward the shower's 60-100+/hr dark-sky peak after midnight.
  6. Orion Nebula / M42 (binoculars) - max 31.8 deg at 00:01; small greenish-grey glow around the Trapezium, mild averted vision helps.
