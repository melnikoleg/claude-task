# Gear Plan - 2026-12-13-berlin-geminids

Visual-only session (naked-eye Geminids + 8x42 binoculars on Pleiades, M42, Double
Cluster, M31, per targets.md), at Zootzen (30 m elevation, open agricultural
flatland, unlit, no shelter, per sites.md), mid-December in Brandenburg. Budget is
25 EUR total and requirements.md records it as covering fuel only - "no gear or fee
purchases requested" - and states explicitly that the lack of cold-weather gear is
"a hard constraint on session duration/comfort for downstream planning, not an item
to be purchased." sky-forecast.md could not pull live numbers for either candidate
night (83-84 days beyond the Open-Meteo horizon) and is NO-GO pending a recheck, but
flags on physical grounds that a below-5 C cold warning "should be assumed likely"
for mid-December Brandenburg after dark. This plan treats that as the operative
weather driver in the absence of live numbers.

## Night Checklist
Grouped for the field. Items marked (owned) are already on the owned-gear list;
items marked (home, no cost) are ordinary household items to bring rather than
gear-shop purchases, since the 25 EUR budget is committed to fuel.

**Optics**
- 8x42 binoculars (owned) - Pleiades, M42, Double Cluster, M31
- Lens cloth or soft cloth (home, no cost) - Zootzen sits near open water (sites.md
  flags fog risk near both candidate sites) and mid-December nights commonly drop to
  the dew point after dark; wipe binocular objectives if they mist over. No dew
  heater is recommended - it is a purchase against a zero-EUR gear budget - so plan
  to wipe rather than prevent, and keep the binoculars in a jacket pocket or the car
  between uses to slow dew formation.

**Power**
- Charged phone (home, no cost) - required: red-light astronomy app/screen filter,
  navigation to the unlit Zootzen access track, and emergency contact given the site
  has no lighting or shelter (sites.md)
- Phone kept warm in an inner pocket rather than an outer bag (home, no cost) -
  batteries lose charge faster below 5 C, the cold threshold sky-forecast.md assumes
  is likely for mid-December Brandenburg after dark; the binoculars need no
  batteries, so the phone is the only powered device in this visual-only plan

**Warmth**
- All the layers already at home worn together under the summer jackets - thermal
  base layer, extra jumpers, warmest trousers, hat, gloves, thick socks (home, no
  cost) - driven by sky-forecast.md's assumed below-5 C mid-December night and
  requirements.md's note that summer jackets alone are a hard constraint on how long
  the party can stay out
- A blanket or sleeping bag from home to lie on/wrap up in during the reclined
  meteor watch (home, no cost) - the observing order (targets.md) has the party lying
  back from 21:00 through past 02:00, well into the coldest hours
- A thermos of a hot drink from home (home, no cost) - warms the party through the
  multi-hour Geminid vigil without any purchase

**Safety**
- Red light / phone screen with a red-light filter (owned via phone) - preserves
  dark adaptation on the unlit Zootzen track and benches (sites.md)
- Sturdy, grippy footwear already owned (home, no cost) - sites.md flags the
  unlit rural access track as potentially muddy or icy in mid-December
- Fully charged phone with offline map/coordinates saved before leaving Berlin
  (home, no cost) - Zootzen has no lighting, shelter or fallback if conditions
  worsen (sites.md)

**Comfort**
- Something to sit or lie on - a car blanket, folding chair or picnic mat already
  owned (home, no cost) - Zootzen has benches and a table (sites.md) but the reclined
  meteor-watching style (targets.md) benefits from a mat/blanket on the ground or in
  the car
- Snacks from home (home, no cost) - sustains a multi-hour session (18:00 to past
  02:00, targets.md's observing order)

No children in this party (requirements.md: "two adults"), so no child-specific
warmth/occupation items apply.
