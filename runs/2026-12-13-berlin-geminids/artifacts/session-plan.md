# Stargazing Session Plan - 2026-12-13-berlin-geminids

## Summary
Two adults drive from Berlin to the Zootzen observation site in the Sternenpark
Westhavelland to watch the Geminid meteor shower, naked eye, with 8x42 binoculars for
a few bright deep-sky targets on the side. Primary night is 2026-12-13, dark window
17:59-06:02, moon a thin 20.2%-illuminated crescent that sets at 20:01 and does not
interfere with the shower. **This plan is currently NO-GO: no cloud-cover forecast
exists yet for this date, and the single decision that will make or break the night is
that recheck within 24 hours of departure.**

## Site
**Zootzen observation site**, Sternenpark Westhavelland (Category 1 point), lat
52.786901, lon 12.609480, elevation 30 m. Straight-line distance 61.5 km from Berlin;
estimated drive 69 minutes each way. Official, sign-posted, no gate, no fee, with an
information kiosk and benches/table for public night use. The final ~300 m of the
gravel access track is reserved for agricultural traffic — park where the public path
narrows and walk in from there. No toilet, no lighting, no shelter beyond the car.
Berlin's light dome sits low on the southeastern horizon; the clearest, darkest sky is
north/northwest, away from it.

## Targets
Ordered as in the timeline above.

1. **The Moon** (naked eye, opportunistic) - thin crescent, 12 deg altitude, gone by
   ~20:01. Does not qualify as a built-around target (0 h above 20 deg both nights) but
   worth the first look.
2. **Andromeda Galaxy / M31** (binoculars) - max 78.6 deg at 19:21; elongated
   grey-white smudge with a brighter core; averted vision helps trace the halo.
3. **Perseus Double Cluster** (binoculars) - max 85.5 deg at 21:01; two dense sparkling
   patches, easily resolved as two separate clusters in one binocular field.
4. **Pleiades / M45** (binoculars) - max 61.4 deg at 22:21; tight, sparkling
   blue-white cluster filling much of the field.
5. **Geminid meteor shower** (naked eye, primary target) - radiant near
   Castor/Pollux in Gemini, high overhead by 02:00-03:00; effectively visible the whole
   dark window; expect real double-digit rates by late evening, climbing toward the
   shower's 60-100+/hr dark-sky peak after midnight.
6. **Orion Nebula / M42** (binoculars) - max 31.8 deg at 00:01; small greenish-grey
   glow around the Trapezium, mild averted vision helps.

## Gear Checklist
**Optics**
- 8x42 binoculars (owned)
- Lens/soft cloth (home, no cost) - wipe objectives if they mist; fog risk near open
  water at Zootzen

**Power**
- Charged phone - red-light filter app, navigation, emergency contact (no site
  lighting/shelter)
- Keep phone in an inner pocket - batteries lose charge faster below 5 C

**Warmth**
- All home layers worn under the summer jackets - thermal base layer, extra jumpers,
  warmest trousers, hat, gloves, thick socks
- Blanket or sleeping bag to lie on/wrap in during the reclined meteor watch
- Thermos of a hot drink

**Safety**
- Red light / red-filtered phone screen - preserves dark adaptation
- Sturdy, grippy footwear - unlit track may be muddy or icy
- Offline map/coordinates saved before leaving Berlin

**Comfort**
- Something to sit or lie on - car blanket, folding chair or mat (Zootzen has benches
  and a table, but reclined watching benefits from a mat/blanket)
- Snacks from home

No purchases or rentals: budget covers fuel only, and gear.md found no gaps beyond
household items above.

## Safety
- **Cold**: summer jackets only, mid-December Brandenburg, likely sub-5 C after dark.
  Layer everything owned at home, bring a blanket/sleeping bag and a hot thermos. Cold
  is the expected limit on how long the session runs, not the clock.
- **Drive home while tired**: session may run past 02:00; swap drivers if either adult
  is cold-fatigued before setting off. 69-minute drive on unlit rural roads.
- **Isolation**: Zootzen has no lighting, no shelter, no toilet, no facilities besides
  benches and a table. The final approach is an unlit agricultural track that can be
  muddy or icy.
- **Phone signal**: save an offline map/coordinates before leaving Berlin; do not rely
  on a live signal at the site.
- **Tell someone**: let a third party know the site (Zootzen, Sternenpark
  Westhavelland), the plan to return by roughly 03:00-06:30, and to check in if not
  heard from.
- **Access track**: do not drive the final ~300 m of the gravel track (agricultural
  traffic only) - park where the public path narrows and walk in.
